## Full description (4000 char max)

You are the last turret standing at the center of the map. The mech tide rises —
wave after wave of robots closing in from every edge. Aim, fire, and hold the
line.

Between waves, spend what you salvage. Bank coins to upgrade your turret on the
fly, and earn cores to unlock permanent power across a deep skill tree. Every run
you push a little further, hit a little harder, and survive a little longer.

**Hold the center**
• 360° wave-survival turret defense — threats come from all sides
• Swivel, aim, and fire; time your shots as the tide thickens
• Stages and escalating waves with tougher enemies and bosses

**Build your loadout**
• Four skill branches — Cannon, Defense, Drone, and Ultimates
• Bullet mods: multi-shot, explosive rounds, guided rounds
• Deploy a combat drone and auto-shooter to share the load
• Layered shield rings and field repair to outlast the swarm

**Unleash ultimates**
• Own many, equip one — laser, freeze, EMP, and warp
• Fire your equipped ultimate when the wave is about to break

**Built to respect you**
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts.
• Play offline, anywhere
• Haptic feedback, adjustable audio, reduce-motion and one-handed options

Defend the center. Outlast the tide.
